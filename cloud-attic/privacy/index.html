3. Was die App auf deinem Gerät speichert

  • Daten für Erinnerungen: welche Arten von Erinnerungen du möchtest, wann die letzte Erinnerung jeder Art kam, die geplanten Erinnerungen und die Zeitpunkte, zu denen du das Spiel in den letzten 14 Tagen gestartet oder nach einer Pause von mindestens fünf Minuten wieder geöffnet hast.   • Google Play Spiele: ob du Google Play Spiele eingeschaltet hast und ob dich das Spiel schon danach gefragt hat (Abschnitt 7).
  • Technische Hilfsdaten: zum Beispiel Zwischenspeicher der Grafik, Bilder für die Benachrichtigungen und ob die App dich schon um die Erlaubnis für Benachrichtigungen gebeten hat. Diese Daten werden nicht gesichert (Abschnitt 5).

  • Settings: language, volumes and frame rate. On first launch, the app uses your device’s language if the game supports it.
  • Data for reminders: which kinds of reminders you want, when the last reminder of each kind came, the reminders that are planned, and the times you started the game, or opened it again after a break of at least five minutes, in the last 14 days. The app stores these times only while at least one kind of reminder is turned on and Android allows notifications; from them it works out your usual time to play, so that reminders come at that time. The app keeps deleting older times, and deletes all of them as soon as you turn reminders off.
  • Google Play Games: whether you have turned on Google Play Games and whether the game has already asked you about it (section 7).
  • Technical data: for example graphics caches, images for notifications, and whether the app has already asked you for permission to show notifications. This data is not backed up (section 5).

The app itself does not send this data; I have no access to it. What goes to Google Play Games if you turn it on is described in section 7. Storing it on your device and accessing it there is technically necessary so that the game keeps your progress and provides the features you use (Section 25(2) no. 2 of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act, TDDDG). Where personal data is processed in doing so, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the game you use); for working out your usual time to play it is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, my legitimate interest being that reminders come at a suitable time. You can turn off reminders in the game at any time.

Deleting: “Reset save” (in the game under Settings → About the game) deletes your progress. To delete all of the app’s data on your device, clear the app’s storage in the Android settings or uninstall the app. A backup in your Google account is not affected by this (section 5).

4. Permissions of the app

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): for reminders, for example when the loot collected while you were away is ready. Reminders are scheduled and shown on your device; no data is sent to me or to a server for them. You can turn off individual kinds of reminders in the game and withdraw the permission at any time in the Android settings.
  • Run after the device restarts (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): so that planned reminders are rescheduled after a restart.

The app requests no other permissions, in particular no internet access, no location, no camera, no microphone, no contacts and no access to your files. Google Play Games needs no permission of the app’s own: the connection to Google is made by Google Play services on your device (section 7). In the version delivered through Google Play, Google adds the licence check permission (com.android.vending.CHECK_LICENSE), with which Google Play checks whether the app was obtained from Google Play (section 6).

5. Android backup and moving to a new device

The app allows the Android backup to back up only the progress, the settings, the data for reminders and the information on Google Play Games from section 3, so that your progress survives reinstalling the app or switching to a new device. Whether anything is backed up is up to you, in the Android settings of your device.

If backup is turned on, Android transfers this data to your Google account and restores it when you reinstall the app or set up a new device. The backup is a feature of Android and Google. Google processes the data as a controller in its own right under the Google Privacy Policy. According to Google, backups on Android 9 and later are end-to-end encrypted if a screen lock (PIN, pattern or password) is set on the device. I have no access to your backups.

In a direct transfer to a new device (by cable or Wi-Fi), the same data is transferred without going through your Google account.

You can turn off backup in the Android settings (depending on the device under “Google” → “Backup”) and delete stored backups in your Google account.
